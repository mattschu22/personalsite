## Cross-agent findings

Seven families emerged:

1. **Control infrastructure (01–03):** excellent protocol legibility, but too close to the existing systems-console direction.
2. **Editorial proof (04–06):** restrained and intelligent; risks returning to the notepad/manual look unless made spatial.
3. **Physical resonance (07–09):** atmospheric and distinctive; the interaction could become visually expensive or obscure causality.
4. **Choreography and textile logic (10–12):** a strong non-diagram way to show quorum; culturally sensitive source material needs care.
5. **Optics and exhibition (13–15):** best balance of clarity, interaction, and visual novelty. Registration/focus naturally explains independent observations resolving into one chosen value.
6. **Civic ceremony and material evidence (16–18):** trustworthy and tactile; risks historical or craft cosplay if rendered literally.
7. **Modular puzzles and morphogenesis (19–20):** most original digital-product direction. Quorum becomes a stable fitted form, and failure becomes an obvious missing cavity.

## Strongest shortlist

### A. Quorum Terrarium — recommended

Use Turing morphogenesis, Soma-cube stability, Game-of-Life locality, and Isotype role clarity. It escapes both technical-dashboard and paper-notebook tropes. The Paxos demo becomes a deterministic field of sockets where accepted blocks physically interlock at quorum. Below the hero, project layouts can use irregular fitted regions without repeating node diagrams.

### B. Quorum Optics

Use stereoscopic registration, Jacob's-ladder propagation, and approachable exhibition design. Competing proposals occupy misregistered planes; quorum snaps three observations into one sharp value. This is the clearest narrative metaphor and can be built with inexpensive transforms and opacity.

### C. Quorum Proof

Use graphic scores, motion studies, communication theory, and case-shifting typography. Five registrar columns accumulate `+` acceptance marks until a broad rule locks. This is the most restrained option and the easiest to keep performant, though it needs spatial depth to avoid feeling editorial again.

### D. Quorum Kiln

Use public counting apparatus, stamped ceramic tesserae, and witnessed cryptographic ceremonies. It communicates trust as a procedure and gives failure tangible weight. Keep surfaces contemporary and avoid historical decoration.

## Recommendation

Build **Quorum Terrarium** with one interaction borrowed from **Quorum Optics**: when three compatible acceptor pieces lock, the previously misregistered value planes snap into focus. Use a matte bone/graphite/moss/oxide palette, a neutral grotesk, one compact mono for ballot IDs, and no global card grid, paper texture, handwriting, terminal chrome, or scroll hijacking.
